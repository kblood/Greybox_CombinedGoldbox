Dispatcher/operand-parser ROLES are established by same-subsystem structural identity against the CoAB oracle, measured directly on Buck's own real GAME.OVR bytes (TPOV overlay, not FBOV -- the first structural-match run against this overlay shape). Per-opcode word sets are measured from each handler's own operand-parser prefetch call (mov al,N; push ax; lcall <derived target>), not inferred from corpus statistics or assumed from another title's table. Opcodes above the CoAB oracle's own 0x00-0x40 range are Buck-specific extensions with no oracle counterpart; their measured word sets are reported but their MNEMONICS/semantics are not claimed.
