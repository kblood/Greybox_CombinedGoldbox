## Claim Boundary

Dispatcher/operand-parser ROLES are established by same-subsystem structural identity against the CoAB oracle, measured directly on Matrix Cubed's own real GAME.OVR bytes (FBOV overlay, confirmed at offset 0 -- refused if not). Per-opcode word sets are measured from each handler's own operand-parser prefetch call (mov al,N; push ax; lcall <derived target>), not inferred from Countdown's dialect, from corpus statistics, or assumed because the two titles share an engine generation. The sibling comparison against Countdown to Doomsday's own measured dialect is a SEPARATE, independently derived measurement on a SEPARATE binary; agreement is evidence, not an assumption this tool bakes in. Opcodes above the CoAB oracle's own 0x00-0x40 range are extensions with no oracle counterpart; their measured word sets are reported but their MNEMONICS/semantics are not claimed beyond what Countdown's own dialect already names them.
